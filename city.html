Berlingo, Italy

A hidden gem in the province of Brescia, Lombardy

Fun Fact

Berlingo is a tiny comune (municipality) in the province of Brescia, in the Lombardy region of northern Italy. The residents are called Berlinghesi! Nestled among rolling hills and lush vineyards, this little town is home to the stunning Church of San Giovanni Battista and is known for its picturesque landscapes and traditional Lombard cuisine served at local trattorias.

A Peaceful Retreat

Despite being a small village, Berlingo is surrounded by scenic walking trails, and visitors can enjoy outdoor activities like boating and hiking in the surrounding countryside. Its neighbors include the towns of Lograto, Maclodio, and Travagliato. It's the kind of place where you go to slow down and enjoy life the Italian way.
