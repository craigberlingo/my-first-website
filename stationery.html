Berlingo Stationery

Office supplies with personality

Fun Fact

Berlingo is a major stationery brand with the slogan "Your Way" — and they have an adorable mascot! It's a minimalist character that looks like an abstract stationery item with legs and big eyes. The mascot appears across all their packaging and marketing, giving office supplies a surprising amount of personality.

A Global Brand

Berlingo stationery is produced in the best factories across Europe and Asia using environmentally friendly materials and modern technologies. After a major rebranding effort, Berlingo positioned itself as the #1 stationery brand in its market, becoming a benchmark that competitors try to follow. Their products are designed to cater to the unique preferences of each customer — from students to professionals.
